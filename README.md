# My Favorite Anime: Demon Slayer

## About Demon Slayer
**Demon Slayer** is my favorite anime because it has amazing fight scenes, interesting characters, and an emotional story.

## Story
The story is about *Tanjiro Kamado*, a boy who becomes a demon slayer after his family is attacked by demons. He wants to find a way to turn his sister Nezuko back into a human.

## My Favorite Characters
1. Tanjiro Kamado
2. Nezuko Kamado
3. Zenitsu Agatsuma
4. Inosuke Hashibira
5. Giyu Tomioka
